# Pinboard

**Discover ideas. Share inspiration. Build your collection.**

Pinboard is a social inspiration platform where users can discover visual content, share their own posts, and interact with a community. Browse ideas across different categories, explore posts, and create your own space for inspiration.

## Live Demo

[Visit Pinboard](https://pinboard-luka.netlify.app/)

## GitHub Repository

[View Source Code](https://github.com/lxka806/Pinboard)

## Features

- **User Authentication** — Register, log in, and log out securely.
- **Explore Feed** — Browse posts shared by the community.
- **Categories** — Filter posts by Photography, Travel, Food, Design, Nature, Technology, and Other.
- **Search** — Find posts by title, description, username, or category.
- **Create Posts** — Upload images and add titles, descriptions, and categories.
- **Post Details** — Open individual posts and view their content.
- **Likes** — Like and unlike posts.
- **Comments** — Add and manage comments on posts.
- **User Profiles** — View and edit your profile.
- **Responsive Design** — A clean interface designed for different screen sizes.
